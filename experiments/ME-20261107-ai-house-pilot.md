## Production bible / constraints

- Stage: one consistent home kitchen, late-afternoon natural warm light, fixed small smart speaker on the counter, one tablet, one cup; no unnecessary room changes.
- Characters: Maya = composed, quick micro-expressions, dry humor; Ethan = overconfident and gradually panicked; the AI = calm off-screen voice plus speaker-light cue. Preserve **existing approved character designs**; do not invent their looks or clone a real person's voice.
- Acting priorities: eye contact, reaction hold after every AI answer, sharp difference between Maya's calmness and Ethan's escalating expressions. No static talking heads.
- Camera: one wide establishing shot, alternating expressive medium/close shots, motivated restrained push-in for peak anger; avoid random camera movement.
- Sound: performance-led dialogue, one subtle UI beep per speaker answer; no assumed licensed background music.
- Captions: accessible English, short chunks, high contrast and outside platform controls. Keep all text out of the generative footage prompts; add in edit.
- Technical quality gates: face/voice identity consistency; lip sync; hand/tablet continuity; first-frame readable conflict; no duplicate or shifting props; reaction timing not compressed; punchline intelligible.
- Budget: unknown and not authorized; provider/model/asset/cost fields remain null until a later approved production run.
