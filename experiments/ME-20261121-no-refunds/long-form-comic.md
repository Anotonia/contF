# No Refunds: The Potluck Clause / 《概不退款：聚餐条款》

**Maya & Ethan · Original long-form panel comic · v0.1**

Six story pages, 36 panels. English master and Simplified Chinese lettering. Read left to right, top to bottom.

**Lineage:** The opening reworks the coupon, tiny print, and Biscuit's refusal from the [18-second Short](../ME-20261121-no-refunds.md). The potluck, handoff, recovery, and ending are new; this is not another variant in the Short’s framing test. The comic uses new character designs made for this draft, not established video reference art. No video, publication, or paid generation.

## Art direction

- **Maya:** angular black bob, amber earrings, mustard cardigan. An eyebrow or a pause does more than a grimace. She never rescues Ethan by quietly taking over.
- **Ethan:** loose auburn quiff, round glasses, teal overshirt. Starts with his chest out; ends with rolled sleeves and wet cuffs.
- **Biscuit:** small cream-and-caramel dog, floppy ears, blue collar. Judges with stillness; willingly cooperates when his turn comes.
- **Stage and props:** one apricot-and-teal kitchen off a purple landing; the yellow coupon book, red stamp, fridge list and blue dog bowl recur. Track the clipped paper corner from the muddy doorway to Ethan's new checklist. The kitchen shifts from warm afternoon to indigo before the amber potluck.
- **Lettering:** speaker lines go in bubbles; `Prop` is in-world text; `Caption` is a time cue or sound. Keep panel 06 silent and hold on Ethan and the rejected book. Keep panel 31 silent and let the mud-free landing show what has changed.

## Page 1 — Fine Print | 小字条款

01. **The offer.** At the kitchen counter Ethan slides a yellow coupon book toward Maya. Biscuit watches from under the counter.
    - Ethan: I got you a very practical gift. | 我送你个特别实用的礼物。
    - Prop: ONE FREE DISHWASH | 免费洗碗一次
02. **Redemption.** Maya tears the top coupon off and presents it back at once.
    - Maya: Perfect. Potluck's at six. | 正好。六点聚餐，这张我用了。
03. **The loophole.** Extreme close-up: Ethan's fingertip reveals the tiny red line on the coupon.
    - Prop: EXPIRES WHEN PRESENTED | 一经出示，立即过期
04. **Due diligence.** Maya fans the remaining pages. Every page has the same stamp. Ethan leans in as though this is completely normal.
    - Maya: They all say that. | 每张都这么写。
    - Ethan: Consistent service. | 服务始终如一。
05. **Second customer.** Ethan crouches and offers the intact book to Biscuit. The dog takes it by one corner.
    - Ethan: You look like a man who reads the terms. | 一看你就是会读条款的狗。
06. **Rejected.** Biscuit drops the book at Ethan's feet and walks off. Maya and Ethan both watch it hit the floor.

## Page 2 — The Hand-Off | 交接

07. **Counteroffer.** Maya flips the rejected book over and sets the block potluck sign-up card beside it.
    - Maya: If the coupon’s no good, how about the real thing? | 券不能用，那来真的？
08. **The job.** She turns the sign-up toward Ethan: their names are beside HOST.
    - Maya: You host tonight. Start to finish. | 今晚你来招待，从头到尾。
    - Prop: BLOCK POTLUCK · 6:00 | 邻里百家宴 · 6 点
09. **Overconfidence.** He snaps an apron on like a superhero cape.
    - Ethan: It’s just dishes with an audience. | 不就是当着大家的面洗碗嘛。
10. **A real hand-off.** Maya indicates the fridge list, coats on the hook behind her.
    - Maya: The list is on the fridge. I’ll be next door. | 清单在冰箱上。我去隔壁。
11. **What he doesn’t read.** The list hangs under a magnet. Visually emphasize its second line with a little blue paw magnet.
    - Prop: 12 BOWLS / TOWEL BY DOOR / WATER FOR BISCUIT | 12 个碗／门边放毛巾／给 Biscuit 加水
12. **Job one, according to Ethan.** With the list behind him, he carefully stamps the first coupon.
    - Ethan: First, a clear returns policy. | 第一步：明确退款政策。
    - Prop: NO REFUNDS | 概不退款

## Page 3 — Six Short | 还差六个

13. **The cupboard.** At 5:42 Ethan finds one lonely bowl where a stack should be.
    - Caption: 5:42 PM | 下午 5:42
    - Ethan: That’s a very small twelve. | 这十二个缩水得有点厉害。
14. **The sink.** Five dirty bowls lean into the sink under a crusted colander. He counts them on his fingers.
    - Ethan: Five in the sink. Still six short. | 水槽里五个。还差六个。
15. **The lead.** His phone lights up with a text. In the doorway Biscuit is coming in from a wet walk, paws visibly muddy.
    - Prop: 2B: Thanks for lending me the big bowls! | 2B 邻居：谢谢你们借的大碗！
16. **The shortcut.** Ethan announces his plan down the purple landing. One neighbor's door is ajar, but nobody is there.
    - Ethan: Great. I’ll just get them back. | 太好了，我去拿回来就行。
17. **The reply.** The phone lights up again. Behind it, muddy prints cross the kitchen threshold.
    - Prop: 2B: On the bus. Back after seven. | 2B 邻居：在公交车上。七点后回来。
18. **Damage report.** Ethan follows the pawprints to Biscuit's empty blue bowl. A damp corner of the coupon book has stuck to one paw.
    - Ethan: Oh. The towel. | 哦。毛巾。

## Page 4 — The Rest of the List | 清单的另一半

19. **Time pressure.** At 5:51 Ethan stands at the threshold with the fallen book and a rag. The doorbell rings early. He freezes.
    - Caption: 5:51 PM · DING DONG | 下午 5:51 · 叮咚
20. **At the threshold.** Maya has returned with a neighbor waiting behind her. Ethan hides nothing: the prints are right there.
    - Ethan: I thought you just remembered where things were. | 我以为你只是记得东西放哪儿。
    - Maya: I remember what’ll happen if I don’t put them there. | 我记得的是，不提前放好会发生什么。
21. **Look again.** Ethan looks from the muddy floor to the empty dog bowl, then up to the list.
    - Ethan: You do this part every time? | 每次你都要提前想这些？
    - Maya: Most times. | 大多数时候。
22. **Ask, don’t punt.** He sets the book down and picks up the rag. Maya stays by the door.
    - Ethan: Could you tell them we need ten minutes? I’ve got the kitchen. | 能帮我说一声，再等十分钟吗？厨房交给我。
23. **Resource.** A small door opens above the sink: a crowded shelf of mismatched mugs, the one thing nobody expects to count.
    - Ethan: Wait. Chili doesn’t know what a bowl is. | 等等。辣炖豆又不认识碗。
24. **A better use for paper.** He tears the remaining unstamped back cover from the book, turns it blank side up and pencils a new plan onto it. The wet corner is still clipped.
    - Prop: WASH FIVE / SET OUT MUGS / WATER + TOWEL | 洗五个碗／摆马克杯／加水、铺毛巾

## Page 5 — Mug Night | 马克杯之夜

25. **Reset the entrance.** Ethan lays the towel flat across the threshold and holds a corner down. Biscuit steps onto it, deliberately.
    - Ethan: Your table, sir. | 先生，您的专座。
26. **Water first.** At floor level, Ethan slides the now-full blue bowl onto the towel. Biscuit takes a drink.
    - Caption: LAP LAP | 啪嗒 啪嗒
27. **Hands in the sink.** Ethan scrubs the last dirty bowl. Five clean bowls dry next to the original one, with six mugs in a mismatched row.
    - Caption: SCRUB SCRUB | 刷刷
28. **The reveal.** The door opens. Warm light spills onto the landing as Ethan offers the first mug of chili to an arriving neighbor.
    - Ethan: Tonight’s bowls have handles. | 今晚的碗自带把手。
29. **A surprising success.** Close on two guests holding their mugs by the handles. A chipped yellow mug and a star-pattern mug clink together.
    - Guest: Can we do it like this next time? | 下次还能这么吃吗？
30. **The real delivery.** Ethan hands Maya a clean mug, then puts a saucer where she can reach it before she asks. The corner of the room is full of clinking cups.
    - Maya: So. Consistent service? | 所以，服务始终如一？
    - Ethan: Working on it. | 正在努力。

## Page 6 — No Expiration | 永不过期

31. **A week later.** Same angle as the first muddy tracks. Now the towel is ready, the landing is clean, and Biscuit waits beside fresh water.
    - Caption: ONE WEEK LATER | 一周后
32. **Before anyone asks.** Ethan checks the back-of-coupon list already clipped to the fridge. Maya is reading on the other side of the counter.
    - Prop: BOWLS ✓ / TOWEL ✓ / WATER ✓ | 碗 ✓／毛巾 ✓／水 ✓
33. **Old habit, new terms.** He slides a single yellow coupon across the counter, echoing panel 01. Maya looks skeptical.
    - Prop: ONE FREE DISHWASH · NO EXPIRATION | 免费洗碗一次 · 永不过期
34. **Make good.** Maya taps her mug, but Ethan is already picking it up.
    - Maya: Do I have to present it? | 我得先出示券吗？
    - Ethan: Nope. I saw the cup. | 不用。我看见杯子了。
35. **The last customer.** Biscuit brings his blue bowl to Ethan and nudges it against his shoe. Ethan looks down; Maya leans into frame.
    - Maya: Repeat business. | 回头客。
36. **The callback.** Ethan bends to refill the bowl. Maya flips the old red stamp face down; Biscuit waits exactly where he was.
    - Ethan: We do refills now. | 本店现在可以续杯。

## Review gate

**Untested hypothesis:** The comic earns the final refill joke if the chores reveal motivates Ethan's change, instead of stretching the refusal gag. Give the lettered comic to a reader unfamiliar with the Short and ask what changed Ethan's mind and why the bowl ending pays off. If the answers only mention the coupons, revise the hand-off and doorway beats before adapting this to video. No audience data or approval claimed.
