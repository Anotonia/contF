# Daily operations — v0.1

## Daily review checklist

1. Read README, AGENTS, ROADMAP, existing issue owners and latest commits.
2. Identify exactly one high-value unknown that affects a real next creative decision.
3. Gather source-backed evidence, including non-hit controls where practical; record timestamps and caveats.
4. If new evidence is meaningful, add or update one bounded research note and relevant issue. Do not churn files without a new fact.
5. Inspect channel data only when explicitly authorized and available; otherwise mark data gap.
6. Propose continue / narrow / pivot / pause / insufficient-data with the reason and next experiment.
7. Never auto-publish, pay for models, expose private analytics, promote a Skill, or modify external video accounts.

## HYPIT-class tool research watch (Issue #7)

- Check the reference-to-variant lab SOP and primary-source project matrix when a **meaningful** code, license, benchmark or technique change arises; log its dated URL, observed versus claimed status and the experiment it alters.
- Keep research about content-production/editing **separate** from evidence that a video performs well on YouTube. No viral-performance claim without matched owned-channel metrics.
- Prefer a bounded, rights-cleared, no-spend bake-off on one owned Maya & Ethan clip over repeated speculative tool lists. No auto-install, paid generation or mass cloning.
- An unchanged watchlist produces “no new evidence,” not a recycled issue or meaningless repository churn.

## Suggested status format

- **New evidence:** source and dated fact, or 'none'
- **Repository change:** precise commit / issue link, or 'none'
- **Decision:** one of continue, narrow, pivot, pause, insufficient-data
- **Current blocker:** data, rights, quality, cost or engineering
- **Next small test:** falsifiable and accountable

A daily task exists for continuous research. Its execution is subject to connected tool availability and access rights.
