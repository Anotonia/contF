# Viral research SOP v0.1

## Objective

Discover *testable structures* from successful and unsuccessful examples, and re-express them as original FT video briefs.

## Procedure

1. **Frame audience/format.** Example: Maya & Ethan English-language family/character/situation Shorts, 20–45 s. Do not mix with technical long-form without a separate cohort.
2. **Collect bounded references.** Start with 10 promising and 10 contrasting examples of similar topic, length, market and post age. Record canonical links and observation dates; avoid redistributing media.
3. **Normalize discovery signals.** When channel history is available, compare views against its recent same-format median at comparable post age. Label unknown channel history and missing denominators.
4. **Analyze content, not just captions.** Track first visual, first conflict, character intent, beat timings, camera shot scale, motion, reaction order, escalation, pause, reversal, payoff and loop.
5. **Propose mechanisms.** E.g. 'reaction delay amplifies a family argument punchline.' Record alternative explanations (audio trend, topic popularity, audience mismatch, timing).
6. **Draft original blueprint.** Preserve abstract grammar, create new premise/dialogue/character choices, and change recognizable creative expression. Check copyright, likeness, voice and platform reuse risk.
7. **Controlled variants.** Create A/B/C hypotheses with one leading changed factor; keep fixed elements explicitly listed. No near-duplicate bulk publishing.
8. **Preflight.** Human script approval → character bible → sample frames → movement/physicality risk → cost estimate → human spend approval.
9. **Measure.** Join publication ID with owned-channel observations at matched windows. Record underpowered conclusions as inconclusive.
10. **Learn.** Version source note, blueprint, test and decision; only promote pattern Skill after repeatable evidence and human acceptance.

## Shot-beat coding legend

- H hook; S setup; C conflict; E escalation; R reversal; P payoff; L loop/callback.
- Each beat: `start_s`, `end_s`, `speaker`, `goal`, `action`, `camera`, `expression`, `sound`, `why_it_might_work`.
- Record exact visual claims as observations; efficacy claims as hypotheses.

## Avoid common traps

- Do not select only the top hits (survivorship bias).
- Do not assume a 'viral score' estimates future success.
- Do not overfit a single video or reuse the same face/song/sequence.
- Do not automate publishing or paid generation from a public-source note.
